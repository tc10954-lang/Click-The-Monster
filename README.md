# 👾 CLICK THE MONSTER — Full Action-RPG Edition

**Play Live:**(https://tc10954-lang.github.io/Click-The-Monster/)

A fast-paced, modular arcade Action-RPG built completely in vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript—featuring custom vector monsters, a 10-track procedural Web Audio synth soundtrack, deep RPG progression, and shareable custom levels.

---

## 🎮 Game Modes
* **⚔️ Standard Hunt (5 Difficulties):** Race the clock across **Easy**, **Normal**, **Insane**, **Nightmare**, and **Hellfire**—each culminating in a multi-phase **Overlord Boss** fight.
* **🗺️ 5-Region RPG World Campaign (25 Stages):** Battle through *Whispering Woods*, *Cursed Crypt*, *Magma Core*, *Astral Spire*, and *The Void Throne* to defeat 5 Region Bosses and unlock permanent Mastery Rings.
* **🌊 Endless Wave Rush:** Survive escalating waves and periodic mini-bosses before the arena hits the 45-monster overrun cap.
* **👹 5-Boss Raid Gauntlet:** Fight 5 unique themed bosses back-to-back with zero swarm filler.
* **💀 Mythic Bounty Contracts:** High-stakes, counter-attacking endgame bosses with Splash Blast banned, reflective shields, cursor-stunning plasma orbs, and exclusive HUD titles (`[☠️ GODSLAYER]`).
* **🛠️ Level & Raid Sandbox:** Build custom swarms and multi-boss raids with sliders and share **Base64 Level Codes** with friends.

---

## ⚔️ Action-RPG & Endgame Systems
* **🎭 3 Hunter Classes & `[SPACEBAR]` Ultimates:**
  * **⚔️ Berserker:** Bonus click damage & crits + *Bloodlust Whirlwind*.
  * **🧙‍♂️ Arcanist:** Bonus Energy regen & blast radius + *Arcane Cataclysm*.
  * **🥷 Shadow Rogue:** Bonus Coin drops & Goblin time-shaves + *Phantom Clones*.
* **🎒 RPG Gear & Upgrade Forge:** Collect randomized *Helmets, Gauntlets, Rings,* and *Amulets* across 5 rarities (**Common → Mythic**) and salvage extras into Scrap to upgrade your loadout.
* **⚗️ Alchemist Lab (`[Q]`, `[E]`, `[R]` Hotkeys):** Brew *Berserk Elixirs*, *Chrono Draughts*, and *Liquid Luck* potions for mid-battle boosts.
* **🔮 6-Branch Ascension Skill Tree:** Rebirth at scaling level milestones (`Lv. 10 → 15 → 20...`) to earn Ascension Points and unlock 18 nodes across 6 specialized sub-branches.
* **🐾 Endgame Vault & Evolution Lab:** Open Bronze, Gold, and Mythic Crates to collect **10 Battle Pets**, evolve them into **🌟 Awakened Cosmic Forms**, and equip **2 Artifact Relics** at once.
* **🏰 Mercenary Guild:** Hire autonomous mercenary squads that earn passive Coins per second—including offline earnings while away.
* **🎲 Chaos Mutators:** Toggle *Lights Out (Flashlight)*, *Mitosis*, *Evasive AI*, and *Chaos Bounce* for `+35%` Coin & XP per active mutator.

## 🚀 Weapons, Controls & Features
* **6 Switchable Weapons (`1`–`6` Keys):** *Blaster (`1`)*, *Rapid Laser (`2`)*, *Splash Blast (`3`)*, *Plasma Boomerang (`4`)*, *Tesla Turret (`5`)*, and *Orbital Ion Beam (`6`)*.
* **🔥 Escalating Combo Fever:** Reach `x25`, `x50`, `x75`, and `x100+` streaks to ignite the arena borders, stack Coin multipliers, and trigger free shockwaves.
* **✨ Rare Variants & Monster Dex:** Hunt 1-in-35 **Prismatic Shinies** and **Radioactive Mutants**, track species kills, and unlock permanent Mastery Perks.
* **📅 Daily Bounties & Lucky Spin:** Date-seeded daily quests and a free daily prize wheel that refresh automatically at midnight.
* **🎵 10-Track Dynamic Synth Soundtrack:** Zero external audio files—100% Web Audio API music that adapts live to menus, difficulties, mutators, bosses, and victories.
